About

B.Tech CSE graduate specializing in AI & Data Science, based in Chennai — building applied ML systems rather than just studying them in notebooks.

Who I am

I'm Karthick, an AI Engineer who likes taking machine learning out of notebooks and into things people can actually use. Most of my work sits at the intersection of computer vision, NLP, and deployment — training a model is only half the job; the other half is wrapping it in something a real user can click, type into, or watch run live.

That's shown up as a YOLOv8 cricket analytics dashboard people can run against live video, a RAG chatbot backed by FAISS and Groq LLaMA that's actually deployed on Hugging Face Spaces, and an NLP tool that helps people find government schemes they qualify for — not flashy, but genuinely useful.

How I work

I lean on FastAPI and Streamlit to get models in front of people quickly, Git for everything from day one, and I'd rather ship a smaller working version than sit on an unfinished bigger one. I'm comfortable across the ML pipeline — data handling with Pandas/NumPy, model training in PyTorch/TensorFlow, and retrieval systems with FAISS and Sentence Transformers.
